Fast flags

Settings

System-level changes. Both are reversible from here.

Pin the Roblox release channel

Writes the channel below to Roblox's own registry key and re-applies it on every launch. Roblox otherwise reassigns you to A/B channels on its own schedule, which is what breaks executors mid-session. Turning this off deletes the key and hands channel selection back to Roblox.

Handle roblox-player: links

Routes the website's Play button through this launcher, so games open in your pinned build instead of Roblox's. Registered under your user account only — switching it off restores the previous handler.

Not included

MAC address spoofing, MachineGuid randomisation and cookie wiping are deliberately left out. Those exist to evade account bans rather than to launch a game, so they are out of scope for this tool.
